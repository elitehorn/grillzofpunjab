Our house

Pure veg, Punjabi fire

Grillz of Punjab is a restro and cafe in Dhampur — built around the tandoor, a vegetarian kitchen, and plates meant to be shared. Marinades rest. Breads blister. Paneer takes the smoke of the coals.

धामपुर का शुद्ध शाकाहारी रेस्त्रो और कैफे — तंदूरी, पंजाबी और चाइनीज़ व्यंजन, थाली और पिज़्ज़ा।

Indian, tandoori, and Chinese under one roof, near Punjabi Colony and opposite Axis Bank. Come for the grill. Stay for the dal, the naan, and a cold coffee.

Questions

Is Grillz of Punjab a pure vegetarian restaurant?

Yes. Grillz of Punjab is a 100% pure vegetarian restro and cafe in Dhampur. The full menu — tandoori starters, chaap, paneer, Chinese, pizza, thali and cafe drinks — is vegetarian.

Where is Grillz of Punjab in Dhampur?

Opposite Axis Bank, near Punjabi Colony, Dhampur, District Bijnor, Uttar Pradesh 246761. A short ride from Dhampur Railway Station.

What are the opening hours?

Open daily from 12:00 PM to 11:00 PM.

Do you offer home delivery in Dhampur?

Yes. Free home delivery on orders above Rs. 499. Call or WhatsApp +91 63953 66055 or +91 74549 51842.

What food do you serve?

Indian and Punjabi tandoori, Chinese, pizza, burgers, thali combos and cafe drinks. Signature plates: paneer tikka, malai chaap, special meal thali and chhole bhature.
